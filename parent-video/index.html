Share your academy story

A short video from a parent helps other families decide. Thirty to sixty seconds, recorded on your phone, is plenty.

What to say

Pick one or two of these. Your own words work best.

  • What was learning like for your child before the academy?
  • What changed once they started? Be as specific as you can.
  • What is one lesson, game or moment your child still talks about?
  • What would you tell another parent who is thinking about trying it?

How to record

  • Hold your phone sideways, in a quiet spot with the light in front of you.
  • Film yourself, the parent. Please keep children off camera for now.
  • Use a first name and last initial only, and leave out school names and addresses.
  • Speak naturally. There is no script, and you can record again as many times as you like.

Permission

Before a video goes on this website, I need your written permission. When you email your video, please include this sentence with your name:

“I am the parent or guardian, I recorded this video myself, and I give Kemp Advisory Group, LLC permission to show it, with captions, on meshayla.com and on its social media and marketing. I understand I can ask to have it removed at any time.”

If you received anything in return for your review, say so in your email so it can be disclosed.

Send your video

Email the file, or a link to it, with the permission sentence above. I will add captions, share the finished version with you, and only publish it once you approve.
