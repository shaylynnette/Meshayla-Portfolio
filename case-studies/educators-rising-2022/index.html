The challenge

The goal was to build a competitive national education leadership program from the ground up, one that could win at the highest level while developing the next generation of educators. That took curriculum, coaching frameworks and performance supports that lifted student capabilities to national level.

What I did

  • Directed the competitive educational leadership initiative.
  • Combined curriculum architecture, performance coaching and leadership development frameworks.
  • Produced measurable, award-winning outcomes for student educators.

The results

In June 2022, four of my TAFE students traveled to Washington, DC, competed against students from across the country, and came home with national placings:

  • 1st place: Public Speaking
  • 1st place (tied): Interactive Bulletin Board
  • 2nd place: Children’s Literature, K-3
“We came, we saw, and we conquered! To say I’m proud is an understatement.”My post the weekend we got home

What came next for my seniors

In May 2022, before the trip, my seniors completed the requirements to become TEA-certified educational aides, and our Superintendent offered each of them a contract to teach at Chapel Hill ISD once they earn their bachelor’s degrees.

Recognized by the district

Before the national trip, the program was already turning heads. In February 2022, The Academies of Chapel Hill named me their first CTE Month spotlight teacher, noting that my TAFE students had 27 competitors advance to the state competition that past December.
