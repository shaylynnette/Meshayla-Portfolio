The challenge

More than 500 professionals across multiple departments had to adopt a new enterprise platform at the same time. Each role had different workflows, different risk levels and different learning curves.

What I did

  • Built matrixed performance pathways in the LMS so users could pick the path that matched their role.
  • Created role-based micro-learning content.
  • Delivered a blended ecosystem that enforced operational compliance milestones.
  • Developed courses in Articulate Storyline and Vyond.
