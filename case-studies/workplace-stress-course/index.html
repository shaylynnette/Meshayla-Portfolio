The challenge

Workplace stress drives burnout, disengagement and lower performance, yet most organizations lack accessible, stigma-free resources employees can use in the moment. The course had to feel approachable rather than clinical and give learners tools to apply during or right after a stressful moment.

What I did

  • Designed an interactive, self-paced Articulate Rise 360 course covering mindfulness, boundary-setting, communication strategies and recovery techniques.
  • Created a companion visual infographic that works as an in-the-moment job aid.
  • Built the six-step framework: Pause & Reflect, Name the Emotion, Check Physical Cues, Identify Triggers, Track Patterns, Take Appropriate Action.
  • Paired the two assets as a blended performance support ecosystem that extends learning beyond the course.
