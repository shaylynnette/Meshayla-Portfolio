The L.E.A.P. Framework: From Analysis to Impact

An original four-step de-escalation framework, built from analysis and turned into a job aid and a full Storyline course.

The challenge

The client had no de-escalation process. Stakeholder, gap, DIF and training needs analyses pointed to the same performance gap: staff had no shared mental model and no in-the-moment language. That meant the answer was a memorable framework, not a policy update.

What I did

  • Ran stakeholder, gap, DIF and training needs analyses to pin down the real performance gap.
  • Created the L.E.A.P. framework from scratch based on what the analysis found.
  • Built and piloted a job aid, then used ChatGPT workflows to redesign it into an enterprise-ready performance support tool with stronger visual hierarchy and behavioral coaching scripts.
  • Built a full Articulate Storyline 360 course that practices the framework in scenarios.
  • Paired the course with job aids, coaching guides and reinforcement tools so training carries into real conversations.

Business impact: higher customer satisfaction, lower escalation risk, more employee confidence and more consistent service.
