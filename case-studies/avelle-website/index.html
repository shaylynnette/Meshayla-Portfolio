Avelle: Hair Brand Launch Site

A product-first website for Avelle, the new brand from Tyler hair stylist Coy Simmons, built to showcase a hair line and send clients to booking.

Status: Launching January 2027

The challenge

Coy Simmons is a hair stylist in Tyler, Texas, and he is changing his company name to Avelle as he adds a hair line and other products. His clients already book at coysimmons.com, so the new site could not replace that. It had to introduce a product brand and still hand visitors to booking.

What I did

  • Separated the jobs: the Avelle site is for products, and it links to coysimmons.com for appointments.
  • Organized the hair line by texture, using the client’s own descriptions: Straight, Body wave, Deep wave, Burmese curl and Kinky straight.
  • Placed founder photos on brand pages and product photos on hair pages, with placeholder imagery until his own photos are captured.
  • Set a clear launch placeholder for January 2027 so the site is ready to go live when the line is.
  • Included his Tyler showroom so local customers know where to find him.

Why it matters

A rebrand can confuse existing customers. Keeping booking where clients already know it, and giving the new products their own home, protects the relationship while the brand grows.
