How I work

The Lesson Plan Method

Every engagement starts the way a good teacher starts a lesson: with the learner. Five steps, in order. Choose one to see what it looks like in practice.

Sound familiar?“We need a course on this by Friday.”

Diagnose First

A training request is not yet a problem to solve. Before anything is built, I find the real performance gap and ask whether training is even the right fix.

  • Performance gap named before anything is built
  • Stakeholder, gap and DIF analyses completed
  • A clear answer to: is this a training problem?
See it in action: Leap, Don’t Escalate →
Sound familiar?“The content is all there, so why is nobody finishing it?”

Design for the Learner

Audience analysis drives every design decision, not the content inventory. I design for the person who is nervous about the new platform, not for the slide count.

  • Audience analysis before learning objectives
  • Role-based paths so people choose what fits their work
  • Accessibility (WCAG/ADA) built in from day one
See it in action: 500 Learners, 500 Roads →
Sound familiar?“They passed the quiz, but nothing changed on the job.”

Build for Transfer

Learning only counts when it shows up in the work. I pair practice with the tools people use in the moment, so training carries past the course.

  • Scenario-based practice built around real situations
  • Job aids and coaching guides that travel with the learner
  • Post-launch reinforcement, such as office hours
See it in action: Two Planes, One Runway →
Sound familiar?“Everyone is using AI, and nobody agrees on how.”

Leverage AI Strategically

Generative AI is a collaborator, not a shortcut. I use it where it speeds the work and keep a human in charge of the result.

  • AI used where it saves time: crosswalks, drafting, voiceover
  • A human in the driver's seat for every deliverable
  • Instructional integrity kept intact
See it in action: Six States, One Map →
Sound familiar?“How do we know it actually worked?”

Measure What Matters

Evaluation is designed in from the start, not retrofitted when someone asks for proof. Measures tie back to what the business or the state needs to see.

  • Kirkpatrick and SAT-aligned evaluation planned from day one
  • Assessments that give reviewers evidence they can verify
  • Findings fed back into job aids and revisions
See it in action: Right the First Time →
